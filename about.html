About

I'm Supratim and this is my blog.

I am a Software Engineer at the intersection of backend systems, real-time infrastructure, and developer tooling. I hold a M.Sc. in Software Engineering from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and University of Amsterdam (joint degree), and a B.Tech in Computer Science from VIT Vellore. Before that I grew up in Muscat, Oman and went to Indian School Muscat.

I previously shipped production React/TypeScript at Mendix (a Siemens company) in Rotterdam, and have two merged pull requests into Tiptap, the 37k-star rich-text editor framework. I'm an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate.

Recently I've been building systems solo and deploying them live: Pulse (real-time API uptime monitoring), Assay (load-testing platform, tested at 120k req/s), Durable Agent Engine (crash-safe Postgres job queue for LLM workflows), and FounderOS (streaming AI product with Stripe billing). In my free time you can find me reading, running, and thinking about system design.

Find me on Github, LinkedIn, and X, or get in touch at supratim347 at gmail dot com.
